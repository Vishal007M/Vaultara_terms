2. Description of Service

Vaultara is an offline-first personal management and reminder platform that provides:

  • Document Vault: Secure local storage of personal documents, IDs, and cards with image attachment, cropping, rotation, and viewing capabilities.
  • Expiry Reminders: Automated tracking of document, passport, warranty, license, and product expirations, sorted by urgency with multi-stage notifications (30, 15, 7, 3, 2, and 1 days before expiry at 10:00 AM local time).
  • Medicine Reminders: Scheduling of medication intake with alarm-level notifications, customizable category sounds, and snooze features.
  • Subscription Reminders: Management and tracking of recurring subscriptions (streaming, utilities, memberships, software licenses) with automatic renewal calculation, sorted by days left, and multi-stage renewal notifications.
  • Data Protection & Backup: AES-256 encrypted manual export and import (.vaultara), as well as automated Google Drive Cloud Sync for upgraded tiers.
  • Tools & Security: PDF export, document sharing, custom notification sounds for each category, and Biometric Lock (Fingerprint/Face).
